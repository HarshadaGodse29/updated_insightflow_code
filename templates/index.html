Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about InsightFlow

How accurate is the speech-to-text transcription?

InsightFlow uses state-of-the-art AI models with industry-leading accuracy of 95%+ for clear audio. Accuracy may vary based on audio quality, background noise, accents, and technical terminology. For best results, ensure good microphone quality and minimal background noise.

What file formats do you support for upload?

We support all major audio formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, and video formats like MP4 and MOV. For real-time recording, we work with any standard microphone input. Maximum file size is 2GB for uploads, with no limits on real-time recording duration.

How does speaker identification work?

Our AI analyzes voice characteristics, speech patterns, and conversation flow to distinguish between different speakers. For optimal results, we recommend having at least 30 seconds of continuous speech from each speaker. You can also manually label speakers in the interface for improved accuracy.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption for all data transfers and storage. Your audio files and transcripts are never shared with third parties. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations. Enterprise customers can opt for on-premises deployment for additional security.

What languages do you support?

We currently support over 50 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, and Arabic. Multi-language support is available on Pro and Enterprise plans. We're continuously adding new languages based on user demand.

How does the free trial work?

The free trial includes 5 hours of transcription per month with access to basic features. No credit card required to start. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time to unlock additional features and higher transcription limits. The free trial renews monthly.
